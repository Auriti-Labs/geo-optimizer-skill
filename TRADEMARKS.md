# Name and logo policy

Status: draft. This policy has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not legal advice. It does not claim that any of the names below is a registered trademark.

## What the MIT licence covers

The source code of GEO Optimizer is published under the MIT licence (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). You may use, copy, modify, fork and sell it, as long as you keep the copyright notice and the licence text in your copy. This policy does not change that.

The MIT licence covers the code. It does not give anyone the right to use the project's names, logos or web addresses to present their own work as this project, or as endorsed by it.

## Names covered by this policy

- "GeoReady"
- "GEO Optimizer" and `geo-optimizer-skill`
- The GeoReady logo and badge artwork
- The domains geoready.dev and app.geoready.dev

These names are used by Juan Camilo Auriti, who publishes GEO Optimizer and GeoReady.

## What you can do without asking

- Say that your project is based on, forked from or compatible with GEO Optimizer, with a link to [Auriti-Labs/geo-optimizer-skill](https://github.com/Auriti-Labs/geo-optimizer-skill).
- Keep the copyright notice and the MIT licence file in your fork.
- Name a fork repository so that it shows it is a fork, for example `yourname/geo-optimizer-skill`.
- Describe the differences between your version and this one, factually.

## What we ask you not to do

- Present a product or service as "GeoReady", "GeoReady Platform" or "the official GEO Optimizer", or in a way that suggests it is operated or endorsed by us.
- Use the GeoReady logo as the logo of your product or in your product's interface.
- Keep this project's links, pricing, sign-up or support addresses in a fork that you offer as your own service.
- Imply that scores, benchmarks or reports from your service were produced by GeoReady.

If you offer a derived product, use your own name and logo, and say that it is based on GEO Optimizer.

## Reporting a problem

If you think a project uses these names or the logo in a way that could mislead users, or you want permission for a use not listed above, write to info@geoready.dev. For security vulnerabilities use the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.
